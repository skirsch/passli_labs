Demo privacy

Northstar is a fictional site demonstrating Passli login and account linking.

Real mode verifies a site-specific Passli public identity and stores its local account binding, provider grant/session association, fictional account state and a bounded site session on the server. It requests no optional profile information. The demo never asks for your Passli password, recovery material or private keys.

An HttpOnly cookie identifies your demo workspace. Its browser lifetime is 24 hours; authenticated site sessions expire after 30 minutes and setup transactions have shorter fixed deadlines. Stored account bindings remain until unlinked or removed by the operator. Unlinking removes the association while retaining the fictional Northstar account.

Northstar passwords and projects are fictional demonstration data. Do not enter real credentials. This is a reference application, not storage for personal or confidential information.
