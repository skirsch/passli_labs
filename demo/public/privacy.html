Demo privacy

Northstar is a fictional site demonstrating Passli login and account linking.

Real mode verifies a site-specific Passli public identity and stores its local account binding, provider grant/session association, fictional account state and a bounded site session on the server. Creating an account requests your name, phone and email through Passli. Only information you approve for sharing is delivered from your authorized device; Northstar does not ask you to retype it. The shared values are retained with the temporary account. The demo never asks for your Passli password, recovery material or private keys.

An HttpOnly cookie identifies your demo workspace. Its browser lifetime is 24 hours; authenticated site sessions expire after 30 minutes and setup transactions have shorter fixed deadlines. Each fictional account, shared name/phone/email, entered email address and local Passli binding expires 30 minutes after account creation, even if you sign in again. Authorization ends at that deadline; a background cleanup removes expired records within 15 seconds, including while the site is idle. Unlinking removes the association earlier. This cleanup affects Northstar only; it does not delete your real Passli account or its provider-side history.

Enter any email address with the public password demo to represent an existing Northstar account. No email is sent or verified; the address is stored temporarily with the fictional account. Northstar passwords and projects are fictional demonstration data. Do not enter real credentials. This is a reference application, not storage for personal or confidential information.
